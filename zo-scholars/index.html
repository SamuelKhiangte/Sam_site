Introduction

About the club — who we are and what we do.

Mizo research scholars te kan pung tial tial a, common platform kan neih lemloh avang hian mahni research bak lo chu kan buaipui lo tlangpui a. Hetiang tawp a kal zel hi tha ti lo in, keini Zo Scholars Journal Club te chuan scientific research a interdisciplinary zia hria in, Mizo scholars hmun hrang hrang a awmte leh line hrang hrang zir mek te phuar khawm tu atan he platform hi kan din a. He platform ah hian talk chi hrang hrang scholars ten anmahni topic chungchan an sawina chance te, networking atan mai bakah future collaborations thleng a rawn thlen thei beisei leh ring chungin hma kan la ve ta rawih mai a ni. Tin, he platform hi researchers PhD emaw postdoc ti mek te tan chauh a nilo a, research lam a kal duh UG emaw PG zir mek te tan pawh join theih vek a ni e.

He platform ah hian a tirah Mizo Scholars hrang hrang ten an research tih lai atangin talk buatsaih a ni anga, heng videos te hi kan website ah leh youtube ah en theihin a awm ang.

Heng bakah hian newsletter, articles leh mahni experience share na platform a ni bawk anga. Tin, research opportunities (Post doc, Internship, Faculty advertisements, Project staff etc) te rawn dah chhuah thin a ni ang.

Join dan tur:

Kan website a mailing list ah hian in sign up tur a ni a. Email ah whatsapp group join na tur kan rawn dah tel ah in lo join theuh dawn nia.
